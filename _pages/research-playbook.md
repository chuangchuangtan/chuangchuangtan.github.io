  <header class="playbook-hero"><div class="playbook-kicker">RESEARCH PLAYBOOK</div><h1 class="lang-zh">我如何做研究</h1><h1 class="lang-en">How I Do Research</h1><p class="playbook-deck lang-zh">两个原则：<strong>回到任务本身</strong>，找到真正值得做的问题；<strong>定义问题，再推导方法</strong>。</p><p class="playbook-deck lang-en">Two principles: <strong>go back to the task</strong> to find the right problem, then <strong>define the problem and derive the method</strong>.</p><div class="playbook-core-flow"><span>Back to the Task</span><b>→</b><span>Ideal Vision</span><b>→</b><span>Gap</span><b>→</b><span class="core">Problem</span><b>→</b><span class="core">Method</span></div></header>
  <section class="playbook-principle"><div class="playbook-index">01</div><div class="playbook-principle-body"><div class="playbook-eyebrow">BACK TO THE TASK</div><h2 class="lang-zh">不要沿着论文往后追，要沿着任务往回看。</h2><h2 class="lang-en">Don't follow the papers. Follow the task.</h2><p class="lang-zh">论文会不断告诉我们“别人现在怎么做”。但一个简单高效的方式，是暂时放下现有方法，先为任务提出一个<strong>清晰、明了的理想愿景</strong>。</p><p class="lang-en">Papers keep telling us how others currently solve a task. A simple alternative is to temporarily set methods aside and formulate a <strong>clear ideal vision</strong> of what the task should ultimately become.</p><div class="playbook-split"><div class="playbook-deadend"><small class="lang-zh">沿着已有论文</small><small class="lang-en">Follow the papers</small><div class="paper-stack"><i>Paper A</i><b>→</b><i>Paper B</i><b>→</b><i>Paper C</i><b>→</b><i>+0.8%</i></div><em class="lang-zh">局部优化</em><em class="lang-en">Local optimization</em></div><div class="playbook-better"><small class="lang-zh">回到任务本身</small><small class="lang-en">Return to the task</small><div class="vision-stack"><i>THE TASK</i><b>→</b><i>Ideal Vision</i><b>→</b><i>What is missing?</i><b>→</b><i>NEW PROBLEM</i></div><em class="lang-zh">从理想终点倒推今天</em><em class="lang-en">Work backward from the ideal</em></div></div><div class="playbook-rule lang-zh"><strong>知道理想情况是什么样，就可以倒推：</strong>哪些还没做好？差距在哪里？现在该做什么？</div><div class="playbook-rule lang-en"><strong>Once the ideal is clear, work backward:</strong> What is missing? Where is the gap? What should we do now?</div><div class="playbook-example"><div class="playbook-example-tag">EXAMPLE · AIGC FORENSICS</div><div class="playbook-aigc"><div class="playbook-aigc-today"><small>TODAY</small><strong>Image</strong><b>↓</b><span>Detector</span><b>↓</b><em>Real / Fake</em></div><div class="playbook-aigc-question"><span>?</span><p class="lang-zh">真正解决这个任务，最终只需要一个二分类标签吗？</p><p class="lang-en">If the task were truly solved, would a binary label be enough?</p></div><div class="playbook-aigc-ideal"><small>IDEAL</small><strong>Image</strong><b>↓</b><div><span>Source</span><span>Where?</span><span>What?</span><span>Why?</span></div><b>↓</b><em>Evidence</em></div></div><p class="lang-zh"><strong>理想愿景与当前能力之间的差距，就是问题的来源。</strong></p><p class="lang-en"><strong>The gap between the ideal vision and today's capability is where new problems come from.</strong></p></div></div></section>
  <section class="playbook-principle second"><div class="playbook-index">02</div><div class="playbook-principle-body"><div class="playbook-eyebrow">DEFINE THE PROBLEM, DERIVE THE METHOD</div><h2 class="lang-zh">现象不是问题。问题清楚，方法才清楚。</h2><h2 class="lang-en">A phenomenon is not a problem. A clear problem leads to a clear method.</h2><div class="playbook-equations"><span>Phenomenon ≠ Problem</span><span>Problem → Method</span><span class="strong">Problem &gt; Method</span></div><div class="playbook-tree"><div class="tree-top"><span>🍂</span><strong class="lang-zh">树叶落了</strong><strong class="lang-en">Leaves are falling</strong><small>PHENOMENON</small></div><div class="tree-arrow">↓</div><div class="tree-mid"><span>💧 <b class="lang-zh">水分</b><b class="lang-en">Water</b></span><span>🌡 <b class="lang-zh">温度</b><b class="lang-en">Temperature</b></span><span>☀ <b class="lang-zh">光照</b><b class="lang-en">Light</b></span></div><div class="tree-arrow"><small>DEFINE THE PROBLEM</small> ↓</div><div class="tree-bottom"><div><small>PROBLEM A</small><strong class="lang-zh">是否缺水？</strong><strong class="lang-en">Water deficiency?</strong><b>↓</b><span class="lang-zh">改变浇水量</span><span class="lang-en">Vary water</span><em>METHOD A</em></div><div><small>PROBLEM B</small><strong class="lang-zh">是否低温？</strong><strong class="lang-en">Low temperature?</strong><b>↓</b><span class="lang-zh">改变温度</span><span class="lang-en">Vary temperature</span><em>METHOD B</em></div><div><small>PROBLEM C</small><strong class="lang-zh">是否缺光？</strong><strong class="lang-en">Insufficient light?</strong><b>↓</b><span class="lang-zh">改变光照</span><span class="lang-en">Vary light</span><em>METHOD C</em></div></div></div><div class="playbook-contrast"><div><small>METHOD FIRST</small><p class="lang-zh">看到现象 → Attention？Agent？新 Loss？</p><p class="lang-en">Phenomenon → Attention? Agent? New loss?</p></div><b>VS</b><div class="good"><small>PROBLEM FIRST</small><p>Phenomenon → Elements → Properties → <strong>Problem → Method</strong></p></div></div><blockquote><span class="lang-zh">问题越模糊，方法越多；问题越清楚，方法越确定。</span><span class="lang-en">The vaguer the problem, the larger the method space. The clearer the problem, the more constrained the method.</span></blockquote></div></section>
  <section class="playbook-signature"><div class="signature-label">THE WHOLE PLAYBOOK</div><div class="signature-flow"><span>Task</span><b>→</b><span>Ideal Vision</span><b>→</b><span>Gap</span><b>→</b><span>Phenomenon</span><b>→</b><span>Elements & Properties</span><b>→</b><span class="focus">Problem</span><b>→</b><span class="focus">Method</span><b>→</b><span>Evidence</span></div><h3>Back to the Task. Define the Problem. Derive the Method.</h3><p class="lang-zh">从任务的理想形态倒推问题，从清晰定义的问题推导方法。</p><p class="lang-en">Work backward from the task's ideal form to find the problem; derive the method from a precisely defined problem.</p></section>
