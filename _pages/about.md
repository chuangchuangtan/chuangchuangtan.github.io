<section class="home-section" id="research">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <span class="section-index">01</span>
    <div>
      <div class="section-eyebrow">Research</div>
      <h2>Research themes</h2>
      <p>I am interested in the reliability of visual information—from detecting generated artifacts to reasoning about semantic, physical, and factual inconsistencies.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="research-grid">
    <article class="research-card">
      <div class="research-card-no">01</div>
      <h3>Generalizable AIGC Forensics</h3>
      <p>Learning transferable forensic evidence that remains effective across unseen generators, post-processing, and open-world distributions.</p>
      <div class="research-line">LGrad → FreqNet → NPR → C2P-CLIP</div>
      <div class="research-keywords">Gradients · Frequency · Generator Priors · VLMs</div>
    </article>

    <article class="research-card research-card--featured">
      <div class="research-card-no">02</div>
      <h3>Explainable & Agentic Forensics</h3>
      <p>Moving beyond a binary label toward localized evidence, structured explanations, and agentic forensic reasoning.</p>
      <div class="research-line">ForenDeX · AnomReason</div>
      <div class="research-keywords">MLLMs · Multi-Agent Systems · Explainability</div>
    </article>

    <article class="research-card">
      <div class="research-card-no">03</div>
      <h3>Trustworthy Multimodal Perception</h3>
      <p>Connecting visual observations with physical knowledge, commonsense relations, and external evidence for robust visual understanding.</p>
      <div class="research-line">Evidence → Reasoning → Verification</div>
      <div class="research-keywords">Physical Priors · Commonsense · Evidence Grounding</div>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

      <div class="section-eyebrow">About</div>
      <h2>About me</h2>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="about-grid">
    <div class="about-copy">
      <p>I am a <strong>Lecturer and Siyuan Postdoctoral Fellow</strong> at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Jiaotong University (BJTU). I received my Ph.D. degree from BJTU in 2025 under the supervision of <a href="http://mepro.bjtu.edu.cn/zhaoyao/c_index.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Yao Zhao</a>. I was a visiting researcher at <strong>A*STAR, Singapore</strong>, in 2023 and conducted research at <strong>Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA)</strong> from 2024 to 2025.</p>
      <p>My long-term goal is to develop visual intelligence systems that can not only make reliable decisions, but also explain <em>what evidence they rely on</em> and <em>why the evidence is trustworthy</em>.</p>
    </div>
